Hidden Wonders is a YouTube channel of short videos and documentaries about remarkable places and true stories from around the world. Watch it at youtube.com/@HiddenWonders-World.

Hidden Wonders Uploader is the channel's own publishing tool. It runs only on the channel owner's computer and uses the YouTube Data API to upload the channel's videos, set their titles and descriptions, schedule them, and post, pin and like comments on the channel's own videos. It is not offered to the public and does not access anyone else's account.
